Here's how to approach this

Under Indian tenancy practices and general civil law principles, a landlord cannot arbitrarily withhold a tenant's security deposit unless there is proven, documented physical damage beyond normal wear and tear or unpaid utility dues specified in your registered or notarized agreement.

Security Deposit Return Clause

Prescribes refund of deposit within 30 days of possession handover after deducting verifiable, documented utility dues or structural repairs.
